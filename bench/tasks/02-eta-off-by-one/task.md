`estimate_delivery` is one business day late for some transfer jobs. A 500 GB standard transfer job started on Monday 2024-03-04 should arrive on Tuesday 2024-03-05, because a standard link moves 500 GB per business day, but we get Wednesday 2024-03-06. The same thing happens with 1000 GB standard, 750 or 1500 GB priority, 400 or 800 GB bulk, and so on.

Please fix it. `transfer_business_days` should return the number of business days the link needs: a day that is only partly used counts as a whole day, and a day that is exactly used up counts as one day, not two. Results for amounts that are not an exact multiple of the daily gigabytes must not change. Fractional gigabytes are allowed, so 500.0 GB takes 1 day and 500.5 GB takes 2.
