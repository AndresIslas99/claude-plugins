`estimate_delivery` is one business day late for some shipments. A 500 mile standard shipment picked up on Monday 2024-03-04 should arrive on Tuesday 2024-03-05, because a standard truck covers 500 miles per business day, but we get Wednesday 2024-03-06. The same thing happens with 1000 miles standard, 750 or 1500 miles expedited, 400 or 800 miles bulk, and so on.

Please fix it. `transit_business_days` should return the number of business days the truck needs: a day that is only partly used counts as a whole day, and a day that is exactly used up counts as one day, not two. Results for distances that are not an exact multiple of the daily miles must not change. Fractional miles are allowed, so 500.0 miles takes 1 day and 500.5 miles takes 2.
